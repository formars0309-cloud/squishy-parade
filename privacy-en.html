SlimeLine Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026.

Records on your device

Adventure and walking island progress, outfits, the village, slime names, melodies, and screenshots are stored on this device. There is no game account. These records are not sent to a game operator server. Game records are kept until you delete them or uninstall the app. In the web version, you can also delete records by clearing this site's stored data.

Purchases and restoration

Google Play processes payments. The app keeps product and purchase status, the original purchase data including its purchase token, and its signature in app-private storage to check access rights. We do not receive payment details such as card numbers. Restore purchases only restores access to the full game. Purchase proof is kept while needed to check access rights. The copy on your device is deleted when a successful Google Play query shows that the purchase is no longer owned, or when you uninstall the app. Google Play retains its transaction records separately under Google's policies.

Exporting backups and photos

After an adult check, you can save a backup or photo to a location you choose. Backups include names and photos. Names may appear on signs in photos, so please use nicknames rather than real names. If you choose a cloud location, that service stores the file.

Deletion and changing devices

Delete records in Settings removes progress, names, outfits, the village, and photos. After uninstalling the app or changing devices, import a backup you previously exported to recover your records. Delete exported files directly from their save locations. Deleting records does not remove purchase rights.

Permissions and children

The app does not use camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions. It includes no ads or usage analytics tools. Purchases, external links, backup and photo exports, and record deletion require an adult check.

Protecting records

Records are kept in your device's app or browser storage, using the access restrictions provided by the operating system and browser. We verify purchase proof signatures and do not include purchase tokens or signatures in manual backup files or game screens. The app does not add file encryption, so please secure your device with a lock and protect any files you export.

Support and privacy requests

If you email Codole, we receive your email address and message to respond to your request. We delete them after resolving the request and completing necessary follow-up, unless retention is required by law. You can send a request to delete your personal information to the same contact email.
